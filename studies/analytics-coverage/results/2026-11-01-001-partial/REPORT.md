## Limits

This is a post-hoc analysis of the responses available when a frozen time limit stopped the run. Completion can depend on effort, latency or answer content, so the partial cells are not guaranteed comparable. The original ten-planned-trial coverage curve is not reported as if unfinished calls had been observed.

Two synthetic cases and one model are exploratory. The planted causes are hidden and not identifiable from the provided summaries; naming another consistent mechanism is not wrong. Mechanism labels and diagnostic scores involve judgment. Requested effort is not a proof of better reasoning. No inference about RL causality, broad creativity, or RNG bias as a validated proxy. The two cases are the task-level units; repeated generations are not independent tasks.

CLI defaults for temperature, top_p, seed and output budget, not exposed or independently verifiable. No overrides. Only requested effort differs between arms; this is a CLI-condition comparison, not an isolated decoding-controlled causal experiment.

Grading was blinded to requested effort, run order, usage and timing. Independent Grok grades were audited by the root coordinator before the treatment key was joined. This is model grading, not human validation. Useful means one consistent mechanism with a diagnostic score of at least 1. Quality averages include every schema-valid answer. No pooled significance test: there are two tasks, not forty independent tasks.
