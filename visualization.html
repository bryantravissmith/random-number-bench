How to compare

Compare versions within a model class while holding prompt, temperature and serving context fixed. Effort labels are requested controls within a model, not a shared compute scale across developers. Model class is a display grouping from requested names; it is not a measured parameter count or training classification.

Intervals describe each fixed answer among accepted trials under a stable, independent-trial assumption. They are pointwise, not simultaneous. Invalid answers and operational failures can affect which trials are accepted. A uniform-looking histogram does not establish independent random draws.

Finite uniform samples normally have nonzero TV. At n=100, the expected empirical TV is about 0.119; at n=1,000, about 0.038. Keep different sample sizes and independent rounds separate.
